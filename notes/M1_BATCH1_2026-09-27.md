I previously reported the 19 missing meal photos as the owner's work. That was

## The batch-1 lesson: diversity beat volume

Of the 14 single-dish candidates, **8 were chole bhuture by the same
photographer**. Accepting them would have produced a beautiful score and told us
nothing — the app would look great at one dish and we would not know how it is
at dal. The kept five are five different meals.

Also rejected: restaurant 3/4 views, a branded hotel thali (ITC Chola), a photo
with a person's arm in frame, a non-Indian salad, an extreme close crop, and
**every multi-bowl thali whose individual dishes I could not name honestly.**

## The measurement, and the two corrections it forced on me

First run, 7 cases x 2: median error 20%, **8 invented dishes**, and one case
off by 37.7%. Both headline numbers were **my labelling errors**:

1. `urad_dal_two_roti` — onion and cucumber were scored as INVENTED. They are
   plainly in the photo; I described them in the note and forgot to put them in
   the truth. The harness was right.
2. `north_indian_dinner` — I labelled it "Chole, Dal, Roti". **There is no dal
   in that photo.** It is chole, a dry potato bhaji, two chapati and tea. My
   reference was ~200 kcal light, which manufactured the 37.7% error. After
   looking at the image full size and relabelling, the same prediction is
   **9.0% off**. The model was closer to right than my label was.

Corrected baseline (7 cases x 2 runs, 14 scans):

| metric | value |
|---|---|
| median calorie error | **19.1%** |
| invented dishes | **4** in 14 scans |
| missed dishes | 6 in 14 scans |
| median calorie/macro disagreement | 5.0% |
| median latency | 4.5s |

## What is actually wrong with the scanner

Two genuine, repeatable misidentifications — both legume dishes, which is a
real pattern rather than noise:

| photo | truth | model says | effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| `kadhi_bowl` | Kadhi | **Chana Masala** | 100% calorie error |
| `north_indian_dinner` | Chole | **Rajma** | one dish wrong |

This is the same failure the September audit recorded as *"paneer
misidentified"* and *"collapsed thali into 1-2 items"*. It is now a number on
a page instead of an anecdote, which is the point of the harness.

Strong cases, for balance: palak paneer 0.0% error, dal makhani 7.1%, north
Indian dinner 9.0%, and the out-of-scope salad probe invented **nothing** — it
did not force an Indian name onto a Western dish.
