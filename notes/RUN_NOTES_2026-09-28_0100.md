I reported **"the scanner is down for every user."** That was **wrong**, and the
owner measured the app's real route working at 01:20 (deepseek-flash, vision,
4/4 correct on a counting test).

never sends that header and walks a different route.** So my "outage" was a
statement about my own probe.

**This is the same mistake as the 11 wrong model ids**: a probe that does not
match the real path, used to make a big decision. Twice in one night, in the
same shape. It is now a rule, not a memory:

> **Never report a product's health from a probe that is not the product's own
> path. Before saying "it is down", make the call the way the app makes it.**

`test/route_aware_labels_test.dart` (5 tests, proven: collapsing the two labels
