# M1 batch 2 — corpus at 10 photos, and the widest measurement so far

**Landed 2026-09-27.** 5 more photos in `tools/ai_accuracy/photos/`,
`baseline_batch2_2026-09-27.json`, `apply_batch2.py`.

## The same trap, caught again

16 candidates fetched. **8 of them were bhindi** — the identical failure mode as
batch 1, where 8 of 14 were chole bhature. Commons returns whatever a dish is
"known for", so a dish-diverse corpus has to be *curated*, not harvested. I took
exactly one bhindi.

Also rejected: two **river photographs** (the search matched the word "parrett"
— a place name, not food), an out-of-focus macro of okra, and four further
bhindi plates. Rejections are recorded with reasons and the files deleted.

## One label came from the image, not the filename

`bhendi_chapati_and_dal_rice_jpg.jpg` **has no rice in it.** The photo is two
chapati, a chopped bhindi sabzi and yellow dal. I labelled what I could see and
recorded in the case note that the filename is wrong, plus that a small dark
item on the plate edge is unidentifiable and therefore left unlabelled rather
than guessed at.

## Measurement — 12 cases, paid production path, 2026-09-27

Producer `tools/ai_accuracy.py`, model `gpt-4o-mini-2024-07-18` via AICredits,
12 scans, **12 answered, 0 rate-limited, 0 retries**.

| | |
|---|---|
| median calorie error | **15.4%** |
| invented dishes | **2** |
| missed dishes | 2 |
| calorie/macro disagreement | 5.2% |
| median latency | 5.1s |
| cost | $0.0040–$0.0047 a scan |

**This is NOT an improvement claim.** The earlier 19.1% median came from 7 cases
× 2 runs; this is 12 cases × 1 run. Different case mix, different repeat count.
Comparing them would be exactly the unnamed-number mistake.

invention rate — which is what you would hope and had no right to assume.

### What is still genuinely wrong

- **`kadhi_bowl` → "Chana Masala", 100% calorie error.** A real, repeatable
  misidentification of a pale yellow bowl.
- **`north_indian_dinner` → "Rajma" instead of Chole.** Both legumes; the model
  is confusing bean dishes.
- **`thali_ma08` 20% under.** It reads the tray but under-weights it.
- **`chickpea_salad_probe` 33.3%** — out of scope by design, and the honest
  limit on non-Indian food.

The two failures are the same failure: **legumes**. That is now a specific,
testable target rather than "the AI is not great".
