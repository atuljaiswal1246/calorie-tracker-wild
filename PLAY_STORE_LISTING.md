## Full Description
Cal Matters makes tracking calories effortless with AI-powered food recognition.

**How it works:**
1. Take a photo of your food or scan a barcode
2. AI instantly identifies items and calculates calories & macros
3. Log your meals and track your daily progress

**Features:**
- **AI Food Recognition:** Snap a photo and get instant calorie/macro breakdown
- **Barcode Scanner:** Scan packaged foods for instant nutrition data
- **Smart Search:** 1,700+ Indian foods database with fuzzy search
- **Manual Entry:** Add meals manually with AI-assisted nutrition lookup
- **Meal Plans:** Save and reuse your favorite meal combinations
- **Fasting Tracker:** Track intermittent fasting with customizable goals
- **Water Tracking:** Monitor daily hydration
- **Weight Tracking:** Log weight with trend visualization
- **Weekly Insights:** See calorie trends, macro distribution, and logging streaks
- **Achievements:** Unlock badges for consistent tracking
- **Groups:** Share progress with friends and family

**Designed for India:**
- 1,700+ Indian foods in our database
- Supports regional dishes and ingredients
- Calorie goals tailored to Indian dietary patterns

**Premium Experience:**
- Clean, minimal design
- No ads, no distractions
- Your data stays on your device

Download Cal Matters and start your health journey today.
