Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 26, 2026

Cal Matters ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Cal Matters mobile application. This privacy policy informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information when you use our application.

Information We Collect

Food Photos

When you take or upload a food photo, the image is sent to an AI service (OpenAI or Google Gemini) for analysis. The photo is processed in real-time and is not stored on our servers.

Account Information

If you sign in with Google, we collect your email address and display name for authentication purposes.

Health Data

We store locally on your device:

  • Body metrics (age, height, weight, gender)
  • Food intake logs
  • Water intake
  • Weight entries
  • Fasting records
  • Meal plans

This data stays on your device and is only synced to your personal Firebase/Firestore account if you are signed in.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide AI-powered food analysis
  • To track your calorie and nutrition intake
  • To display progress and insights
  • To enable group features (shared with group members only)

Data Storage

All health and nutrition data is stored locally on your device using SharedPreferences. If you are signed in, data may be backed up to Firebase Firestore under your personal account.

Third-Party Services

  • OpenAI / Google Gemini: Used for food image analysis. Images are processed temporarily and not retained.
  • Firebase: Used for authentication and optional cloud backup.
  • Open Food Facts: Used for barcode scanning (public database).

Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties. Group data is only visible to members you have invited.

Children's Privacy

This app is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. You will be notified of any changes by posting the new policy on this page.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at: support@calmatters.app
