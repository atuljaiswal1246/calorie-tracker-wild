Delete Your Account

Last updated: August 29, 2026

Cal Matters ("we", "our", or "us") provides you with full control over your account and the data we store. You can delete your account and associated data at any time.

What data is deleted

  1. Account — your Cal Matters sign-in account is permanently deleted and can no longer be used.
  2. Profile data — body metrics (age, height, weight, gender), goals, and preferences.
  3. Nutrition data — your food logs, calorie and macro records, water intake, weight entries, fasting records, and meal plans.

How to request deletion

To delete your account and associated data, send an email to atuljaiswal1246@gmail.com from the email address you signed in with. Include the subject line "Delete my Cal Matters account". We will process your request within 30 days and confirm by email once deletion is complete.

What is not deleted

Food photos are processed in real-time and are never stored, so there is nothing to delete there.

Data retention

After you request deletion, your account and all associated data are permanently removed from our systems within 30 days. There is no way to recover a deleted account.

Questions

If you have any questions about account deletion, contact us at atuljaiswal1246@gmail.com.
